02 View Explorations Report — Partner Link Clicks
Navigate to Explore and open Partner Link Clicks by Group & Contributor
In the left-hand nav, click Explore. From the list of pre-built reports, select Partner Link Clicks by Group & Contributor.

Add dimensions
To add a new dimension: go to the Variables section, then under Variables find Dimensions and click the + icon. Search for and select the dimension you want (e.g. contributor name or link placement), then click Confirm in the top right. Once added, the dimension also needs to appear under rows — go to Settings, find Rows, and select the same dimension there. Repeat for each dimension you want to break down by.

04 Save & manage your custom reports
Find your saved copies in Explorations
The custom reports you have created are saved in your Explorations section in Google Analytics. You can revisit them at any time by navigating back to Explore — your copies will appear in the list alongside the original pre-built reports.
Explorations10
Rename your report so it's easy to find later
From the Explorations list, find your copied report and click the More actions menu (three dots). Select Rename and give it a meaningful name — something that describes the date range or focus of your analysis — so you can easily identify and revisit it in the future.
